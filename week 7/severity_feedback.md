# UI3 forum feedback — severity analysis (Card 2 of 5)

**Source:** `week7_figma_feedback.csv` (Figma community forum posts about UI3 and related editor UX)

**Task:** Identify the three most serious usability problems users describe, rate each as Critical, High, or Medium, and cite specific posts.

---

## Problem 1: Forced migration off UI2 with no durable choice

**Severity: Critical**

**Reasoning:** Removing the interface professionals trained on turns other UI3 issues into a blocking constraint for paid work, and multiple posts describe major productivity loss rather than mild preference.

**Citation:** **OnlyRodas** (id 1, April 2, 2025) — *“It has made my workflow painfully slow and incredibly unproductive… Now that you're FORCING this change… It's a downgrade in usability, efficiency, and user experience.”*

---

## Problem 2: Weaker visual accessibility and noisier layout (especially the right panel)

**Severity: High**

**Reasoning:** Thin type and dense, low-contrast panels increase cognitive load and make scanning properties harder during long sessions—concrete accessibility and efficiency regressions.

**Citation:** **Artist and Machine** (id 7, March 26, 2025) — *“The new UI is not very accessible either — very small thin type… it is not as efficient or functional.”*

**Supporting citation:** **Anastasia Zotov** (id 14, April 9, 2025) — *“…how visually noisy the right panel has become, with all grey backgrounds for inputs, extra fields, and no separation between sections.”*

---

## Problem 3: Primary tools buried or rearranged; expert muscle-memory workflow breaks

**Severity: High**

**Reasoning:** When core actions move into submenus or disappear from familiar chrome, power users report paralysis and constant hunting—high impact for a tool sold on speed.

**Citation:** **Radu Muntean** (id 15, April 10, 2025) — *“Thanks for removing an entire navbar and moving the shape intersect/union and burying it in a submenu…”*

**Supporting citation:** **Darius Vitkunas** (id 11, April 3, 2025) — *“With UI3 turned on I feel literally paralysed… having… checkboxes actually visible when I select them…”*

---
